## 📸 Photo Specifications for Team Members

### When You're Ready to Add Real Photos:

**Recommended Specifications:**
- **Dimensions:** 600 x 600 pixels (square format)
- **File Format:** JPG or PNG
- **File Size:** Under 2 MB (preferably 500 KB - 1 MB)
- **File Naming:** Use descriptive names like:
  - `alecia-jones-headshot.jpg`
  - `shuchi-alam-photo.jpg`
  - `mike-nielson-portrait.jpg`

**Photography Tips:**
- Professional headshot style
- Plain or simple background
- Good lighting, facing camera
- Shoulders and head visible
- High resolution (at least 600x600)

**How to Replace Placeholder Photos:**

1. Go to Sanity Studio → Team Members
2. Click on the team member (e.g., "Alecia Jones")
3. Click on the existing photo
4. Click **"Replace"** button
5. Upload the new professional photo
6. Update Alt Text if needed
7. Click **"Publish"**
8. Changes appear on the website immediately! 🎉

## 🚀 Pro Tips

1. **Changes Appear Instantly:** Once you click "Publish" in Sanity, changes appear on your website within seconds!

2. **Save as Draft:** You can work on content and save it as a draft before publishing.

3. **Preview Before Publishing:** You can preview changes in Sanity before making them live.

5. **Edit Anytime:** Click on any team member or program to edit and republish.
