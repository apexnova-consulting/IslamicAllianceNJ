## How to Upload the Photo

### Step-by-Step:

1. **Access Sanity Studio**
   ```
   Go to: https://www.islamicalliancenj.com/studio
   ```

2. **Create Shop Item**
   - Click "Shop Items" in left sidebar
   - Click "Create" button

3. **Locate Product Image Field**
   - Scroll down to "Product Image" section
   - You'll see an upload area

4. **Upload Your Photo**
   - Click "Upload" button
   - Navigate to where you saved the hoodie photos
   - Select your chosen photo (recommend the female model photo)
   - Wait for upload to complete

5. **Add Alt Text**
   - After upload, you'll see "Alt Text" field
   - Enter exactly:
   ```
   Islamic Alliance Hoodie - White hoodie with Islamic Alliance logo and "Ummah in Action" tagline
   ```

6. **Adjust Hotspot (Optional)**
   - You'll see your uploaded image
   - A blue circle appears (this is the "hotspot")
   - Drag it to center on the logo if needed
   - This ensures the logo is always visible in thumbnails

7. **Continue with Rest of Form**
   - Fill in title, description, price, etc.
   - (See HOODIE_SETUP_GUIDE.md for complete instructions)

## Photo Tips for Future Products

When taking photos of new products:

### Do:
✅ Use natural or bright lighting
✅ Use neutral background (white, gray, or simple)
✅ Show product clearly and prominently
✅ Take photos from straight-on angle
✅ Ensure logo/text is readable
✅ Use high resolution camera
✅ Take multiple angles
✅ Include model wearing product (shows fit)

### Don't:
❌ Use dark or dim lighting
❌ Use busy or distracting backgrounds
❌ Take blurry photos
❌ Crop too tightly
❌ Use filters that change colors
❌ Include irrelevant items in frame
