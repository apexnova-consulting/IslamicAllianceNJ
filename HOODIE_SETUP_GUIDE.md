# Quick Start: Adding the Islamic Alliance Hoodie

This is a quick guide to get your hoodie product live on the website.

## Step 1: Access Sanity Studio

1. Open your browser and go to: `https://www.islamicalliancenj.com/studio`
2. Log in with your admin credentials

## Step 2: Create the Hoodie Product

1. In the left sidebar, click on **"Shop Items"**
2. Click the **"Create"** button (or the + icon)
3. Fill in the following information:

**Product Title:**
```

**SKU:**
```

## Step 3: Save and Publish

1. Click the **"Publish"** button in the bottom right corner
2. You should see a success message

## Step 4: Verify It's Live

1. Open a new tab and go to: `https://www.islamicalliancenj.com/shop`
2. You should see your hoodie displayed!
3. Try clicking on a size and then "Order Now" to test the checkout flow

## What Happens When Someone Orders?

1. Customer selects a size and clicks "Order Now"
2. They fill out their information and delivery address
3. They choose payment method:
   - **Pay on Delivery**: They'll pay cash when you deliver (NJ only)
   - **Zelle**: They'll pay via Zelle to islamicalliance.nj@gmail.com
4. Order is submitted
5. **You'll see the order in Sanity Studio** under "Order Submissions"

## How to Check Orders

1. Go to Sanity Studio: `https://www.islamicalliancenj.com/studio`
2. Click **"Order Submissions"** in the left sidebar
3. You'll see all orders with:
   - Customer name and contact info
   - Delivery address
   - Size and quantity ordered
   - Payment method chosen
   - Order status

## Managing Orders

### For Zelle Orders:
1. Check your Zelle account for payment
2. Once payment received, open the order in Sanity
3. Change **Status** to "Payment Confirmed"
4. Click **Publish**

### For Pay on Delivery Orders:
1. Contact customer to arrange delivery
2. Update **Status** as you process:
   - "Processing" - preparing the order
   - "Out for Delivery" - on the way
   - "Delivered" - completed
3. Click **Publish** after each update

## Tips for Success

### Photography
- The photos you provided are perfect!
- If you want to add more photos later, you can edit the product and upload additional images

### Inventory
- If you run out of stock, edit the product and uncheck "Active"
- When back in stock, check "Active" again

### Customer Service
- Respond to orders within 24 hours
- Update order statuses so customers know what's happening
- Use the "Admin Notes" field in each order for your own tracking

## Troubleshooting

**Problem: Product not showing on shop page**
- Make sure "Active" is checked
- Make sure you clicked "Publish"
- Try refreshing the page

**Problem: Can't see orders**
- Go to Sanity Studio
- Click "Order Submissions" in sidebar
- Orders appear newest first

**Problem: Customer says they can't order**
- Check that at least one size is selected
- Verify "Active" is checked
- Ask them to try a different browser
